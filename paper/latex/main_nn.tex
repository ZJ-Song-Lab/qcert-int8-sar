\title{QCert: Making False-Alarm Guarantees Survive Integer Quantization
       for On-Board SAR Ship Detection}

\author[inst1]{作者姓名（投稿时填写）}
\affiliation[inst1]{organization={单位名称（投稿时填写）},
                    city={城市}, postcode={邮编}, country={中国}}

\begin{abstract}
Deployable SAR ship detectors are calibrated in floating point but executed on
fixed-point NPUs --- and the acceptance metric, mAP, is blind to the mismatch:
mAP integrates over the full ranking, whereas the false-alarm rate
$P_{fa}=10^{-3}$ lives at the 99.9th percentile, exactly the region that int8
quantization perturbs through denominator granularity, accumulated rounding,
and potential output saturation. We move the false-alarm contract into integer
arithmetic. First, we derive a closed-form bound that propagates per-layer
scale factors to $P_{fa}$, and show that for deployments with per-position null
models the denominator of the decision statistic dominates --- a 5\%
quantization error on the null-model scale inflates $P_{fa}$ by 1.6$\times$ at
$\alpha=10^{-3}$; the bound is verified on all 12 error levels (our SSDD
instance fixes the null scale, where the output-grid mechanism dominates
instead, \S3.2.3). Second, we give a division-free integer decision rule and a
histogram-based integer quantile estimator, so that the contract holds by
construction on the deployed graph, the host leaves the inference loop, and the
integer threshold is transported across quantization configurations using only
two scale tables --- zero data, zero re-calibration. On an Atlas 200I DK A2
(Ascend 310B) board we report: transported-threshold $P_{fa}$ drift of
0.046--0.111 dex, versus 2.55 dex ($\sim$300$\times$) without scale
conversion; bit-exact reproducibility of the deployed graph (rate 1.0);
P99-latency cross-window spread of only 0.69\% (INT8 rerun; 1.76\% for the
sensor-instrumented FP16 run with flat latency as temperature rose
51.7$\to$53.5\,$^\circ$C) over 40-minute continuous loads (per-window
$P_{fa}$ constancy being a direct corollary of integer determinism); per-scene
integer quantiles cut the candidate load 4.7--4.9$\times$ under rough seas
(transparent bucketer, rough subset of 35 frames), with a 0.024 ms decision
layer (host-simulated upper bound) and a 1.31$\times$ INT8 speedup over FP16;
and end-to-end compatibility with $P_d=0.991$ (7$\times$ the pixel-domain CFAR
baseline) at only $-0.29$ mAP points. These measurements are only meaningful
on fixed-point hardware --- and they show that a threshold should live in the
number domain where it is executed.
\end{abstract}

\section*{摘要（中文）}
可部署的 SAR 舰船检测器在浮点域标定、在定点 NPU 上执行，而验收口径 mAP
恰好看不见这一错配：mAP 是对全排序的积分，误警率 $P_{fa}=10^{-3}$ 是
99.9\% 分位——int8 量化通过分母量化粒度、逐层舍入与潜在的输出饱和恰好只
扰动尾部，在 mAP 上几乎不可见。本文把误警率契约搬进整数算术。首先给出
闭式界把逐层 scale 传播为 $P_{fa}$ 漂移，并定位分母（零模型尺度）为逐
位置零模型部署的主导机制——$\alpha=10^{-3}$ 处 5\% 的量化误差使
$P_{fa}$ 膨胀 1.6 倍；合成验证在全部 12 个误差档成立（本文 SSDD 链路的
零模型尺度固定，主导项转为输出网格机制，见 \S3.2.3）。随后给出无除法整
数判决规则与直方图整数分位数估计，使契约在部署图上按构造成立、host 退
出推理回路，并支持仅凭两份 scale 表的免数据阈值搬运。在 Atlas 200I DK
A2（Ascend 310B）上的板级实测给出：搬运后 $P_{fa}$ 漂移 0.046--0.111
dex，而不换算高达 2.55 dex（约 300 倍）；同一部署图逐 bit 可复现
（bit-exact 率 1.0）；40 分钟连续负载下 P99 跨窗极差仅 0.69\%（INT8 复
跑；FP16 带传感器运行为 1.76\%，温度 51.7$\to$53.5\,$^\circ$C 时延不涨；
$P_{fa}$ 逐窗恒定系整数确定性的直接推论）；逐场景整数分位把粗糙海况候
选负载压降 4.7--4.9 倍（透明分桶器 rough 子集 35 帧）、判决层仅 0.024 ms
（host 仿真上界）、INT8 较 FP16 提速 1.31 倍；端到端检测兼容无损
（$P_d$ 0.991，为像素域 CFAR 基线的 7 倍，mAP 仅降 0.29 点）。上述测量
只在定点硬件上有意义——它们证明：阈值应当活在它被执行的那个数域里。
